# NGSL 1.2

Source for the Top 1000 decks (docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-08-core-1000-words-design.md).

- `NGSL_12_stats.csv` — rank of each lemma (`SFI Rank`).
- `NGSL_12_lemmatized_for_teaching.csv` — each lemma followed by its inflected forms; `##` lines are comments.

Both downloaded unchanged from https://www.newgeneralservicelist.com/new-general-service-list.

Browne, C., Culligan, B., & Phillips, J. (2013). *The New General Service List*.
Licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International License.

`function-words.json` is ours: the lemmas left out of the Top 1000 (articles, pronouns,
prepositions, conjunctions, auxiliaries and modals, determiners, interjections).
